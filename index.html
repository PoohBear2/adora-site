00:27.0

Everyone knows how to film their product. Nobody knows what to film.

Short video is the cheapest acquisition channel on earth and it is a numbers game. Brands that post many variations and keep what works win. Every variation costs an editor, a voiceover, captions, music and a translation, so small businesses post one video a week with a guessed hook and learn nothing.

Agencies charge $2,000 to $5,000 a month. UGC creators charge $150 to $500 a video. AI avatar tools make footage that audiences and algorithms are learning to reject. General editors like CapCut cut for cinema, not for conversion. Adora is the first editor built for one job: find the structure that is converting this week, and cut your footage into it, a hundred times.

01:00.0

Four steps. One afternoon of filming becomes weeks of posting.

01 RESEARCH

Finds what is winning

Write a two line brief. Adora looks up your competitors in the App Store, then digs through every one of their TikTok accounts and hashtags for videos with 50,000+ likes from the last two months. A local vision model reads each one and breaks it into sections: hook, problem, reveal, demo, proof, CTA.

02 FILM

Tells you the shot

Every section becomes one sentence about your shot, your product doing only what the winning video did. A reference clip sits beside each one. You film a few takes on a phone. No editing skill needed.

03 GENERATE

Cuts the variations

Drop the takes in. Adora writes the script to each section's word budget, clones a voice line by line, burns in karaoke captions, generates original music and lands the drop on the reveal, and cuts a new combination of takes every time. Finish one in the live editor, then make all the rest like it. Up to 50 a press.

04 DISTRIBUTE

Posts everywhere

Every approved variation is dubbed into every language your accounts post in, with the same cloned voice, and scheduled across all of them. Losing formats are dropped. Winning formats get more variations.
